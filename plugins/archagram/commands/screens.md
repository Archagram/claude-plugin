---
description: Prepare the screens of a journey in Archagram
argument-hint: "[journey]"
---

Prepare the screens of an Archagram journey, what a person sees at its steps, by following the journey-screens skill from this plugin.

Use the journey the user named with this command, if any. Otherwise list the project's journeys and ask which one. Ask for the project when more than one could hold it, and confirm which device the person uses and which moments they see before writing anything.

Finish with the devices and the screens, the presses that move the journey, and the warnings left.
